        How the math works

        Your pay, bills and savings get turned into an amount per paycheck, so every paycheck looks the same.

        Spending money is what's left after bills and savings. Log what you spend on Today and it counts down until payday.

        Savings that move on their own go up by themselves each payday. Cash you set aside by hand goes up when you check it off on Today.

        The forecast on the Savings tab adds your savings on each payday from now on. It doesn't count interest.

          About Till Payday

          A free paycheck planner

          What it's for

          Making each paycheck last until the next one, and saving a little every time. Till Payday splits each check into bills, savings and spending money, then counts down what's left until payday. It's built for real budgets: one job or two, pay every week or once a month, rent that lands on the 1st.

          Your privacy

            Keep your plan safe

              Free help with money

              • 211. Dial 211 or visit 211.org to find local help with rent, food and utility bills, in the US and Canada.
              • Free tax help. IRS volunteers do free tax returns for people with low to moderate income. See free tax return preparation.
              • A local credit union. Many offer low-cost accounts, small loans and free money coaching, often a safer choice than payday loans.

              Terms of use

              Till Payday is free to use and is provided “as is,” without warranties of any kind. It's a planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice. Check important numbers with your bank, and with your paystub for what you actually take home.

              Built with help from AI (Claude, made by Anthropic). Mistakes are possible, so if a number looks wrong, please say so.
