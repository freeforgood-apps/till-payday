# Till Payday

A free paycheck planner that works on any phone or computer. Make each check last till payday, and save a little every time. No account, no ads, no tracking.

**Open the app:** https://freeforgood-apps.github.io/till-payday/

**Run the self-test:** https://freeforgood-apps.github.io/till-payday/selftest.html

## Who it's for

Anyone trying to make one paycheck last until the next, especially people living paycheck to paycheck. It's built for real budgets: one job or two, pay every week or once a month, rent that lands on the 1st. There's nothing to sign up for and no bank to link. Your numbers stay on your own phone or computer.

## What's inside

- **The paycheck split:** each check split into bills, savings and spending money, for pay every week, every 2 weeks, twice a month or once a month
- **Today:** what's left to spend until payday, and a to-do list for this paycheck: the bills it covers and the savings to set aside
- **Not sure of your take-home?** Estimate it from your hourly pay and hours a week
- **Spending:** log what you spend, see where it went by label over your last 1, 3 or 6 paychecks, and count your no-spend days
- **Savings:** a forecast of what you'll have saved, a savings goal with a rough date you'll reach it, and **Count my cash** to total up the bills and coins you've saved
- **Paying off debt:** your debt-free date, total interest, and what paying a little extra does
- **Can I afford it?** Check a price against what you have left, see it in hours of work, and put online buys on a sleep-on-it list for a day
- **Calculator** that can log its total as spending
- **Calendar reminders** for paydays and bill due dates, as a calendar file or Google Calendar links
- **Example numbers** on first open, so you can look around before typing your own
- **Backups as a file or a code**, with an optional password, plus automatic copies from the last 3 days on your device and a reminder when it's been a while since your last backup
- **A PIN lock** for a shared phone

## Add it to your home screen

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the link in Safari → tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → tap **⋮** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**)
- **Computer:** in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar

On iPhone and iPad, the Home Screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari. Before you add it, copy a backup code (Plan tab → **Back up or restore my plan**), then restore it in the Home Screen app. Safari can clear saved data for websites you haven't opened in 7 days, and the Home Screen app avoids that, so adding it is the best way to protect your plan. Back up now and then too: Till Payday reminds you when it's been a while. The same tips are in the app, under About → Keep your plan safe.

### Run the self-test yourself

Open **selftest.html** in any browser (the app's About screen links to it, under Feedback) and tap **Run self-test**. In about a minute it taps through the whole app and shows a pass/fail report you can copy or email, with the version it checked on the first line. It saves to its own separate storage, so it never touches anyone's real plan. If you run it on a browser that isn't listed above, please share the report by email or in this repository's **Issues** tab (see Feedback below).

## Privacy

- No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Your numbers are saved only in your own browser, on your own device (local storage). Till Payday never sends them anywhere.
- A built-in privacy lock: the browser blocks the page from sending anything over the internet, even if it tried. The self-test shows the lock working in your browser.
- Everything Till Payday needs, fonts included, comes from this one site. No other company is involved.
- A Google Calendar link sends that one event's name, date and amount to Google, only when you tap it.
- **Erase my numbers** on the Plan tab starts over but keeps 3 days of automatic copies, in case it was a mistake. **Delete everything from this device** in About removes those too. Clearing your browser's data erases them as well, so back up now and then.
- Like any site, GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security, but it never sees your numbers; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Accessibility

Designed for phones first, with large tap targets, screen-reader labels, a text size button (**Aa**: normal, large or extra large), light and dark themes that follow your device's setting, and text colors that meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. It works with iPhone's Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion settings, and with VoiceOver. On computers, the keyboard works too: ← and → (or Home and End) move between tabs, Esc closes a panel or the calculator, and the calculator takes number keys, + - * / and Enter. If anything is hard to use, please let us know (see Feedback below).

## Not financial advice

Till Payday is a planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice. Its numbers are estimates: check important ones with your bank, and with your paystub for what you actually take home. Debt payoff and savings interest are estimates too. For a debt, your statement has the exact numbers.

For free help with money (also in the app, under About):

- **211:** dial 211 or visit [211.org](https://www.211.org) to find local help with rent, food and utility bills, in the US and Canada.
- **Free tax help:** IRS volunteers do free tax returns for people with low to moderate income. See [free tax return preparation](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/free-tax-return-preparation-for-qualifying-taxpayers).
- **A local credit union:** many offer low-cost accounts, small loans and free money coaching, often a safer choice than payday loans.

## Updating the app

Follow [`how-to-update.md`](how-to-update.md): edit `index.html`, run `rebuild.py`, then upload `index.html`, `selftest.html` and `sw.js` to this repository and commit. GitHub Pages updates the site within a minute or two, and everyone's saved plan carries over. After an update, run the self-test again and add new rows to **Tested on**.
