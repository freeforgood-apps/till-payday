# Till Payday

A free paycheck planner. Make each check last till payday, and save a little every time.

**Open it:** https://freestudycoach.github.io/till-payday/

- Splits each paycheck into bills, savings and spending money, then counts down what's left until payday.
- Works with pay every week, every 2 weeks, twice a month or once a month.
- Savings forecast, a savings goal, "Can I afford it?", and a sleep-on-it list for online buys.
- No accounts, ads or tracking. Your numbers are saved only in your own browser. Back up any time from the Plan tab.
- Add it to your Home Screen and it works like an app, even offline.

Till Payday is a one-person volunteer project, built with help from AI (Claude, made by Anthropic). It's a planning tool, not financial advice.
