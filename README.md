# Till Payday

A free paycheck planner. Make each check last till payday, and save a little every time.

**Open it:** https://freestudycoach.github.io/till-payday/

- Splits each paycheck into bills, savings and spending money, then counts down what's left until payday.
- Works with pay every week, every 2 weeks, twice a month or once a month.
- Savings forecast, a savings goal, "Can I afford it?", and a sleep-on-it list for online buys.
- Where it went: spending by label over your last 1, 3 or 6 paychecks.
- Debt payoff: your debt-free date, total interest, and what paying a little extra does.
- Calendar reminders for paydays and bill due dates, prices in hours of work, count my cash, and no-spend days.
- No accounts, ads or tracking. Your numbers are saved only in your own browser, with automatic daily copies, checked saves and backup reminders. Backups can be password-protected, and a PIN lock helps on a shared phone.
- A built-in privacy lock: the browser blocks the page from sending anything over the internet. Fonts and everything else come from this site only.
- Add it to your Home Screen and it works like an app, even offline.
- Something not working? Open selftest.html for a self-test that checks Till Payday on your phone.

Till Payday is a one-person volunteer project, built with help from AI (Claude, made by Anthropic). It's a planning tool, not financial advice.
